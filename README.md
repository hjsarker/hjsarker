I'm an IT and data professional whose work spans hands-on technical support, business intelligence, analytics platforms and enterprise systems. I started in IT infrastructure and telecom billing operations, then moved into BI platform support, enterprise migrations, process governance and IT/OT operations in Germany.

I enjoy solving practical problems: troubleshooting services, making reporting dependable, checking the quality of data and building useful tools. Outside my professional roles, I develop and operate independent analytics and AI-enabled products, including **[HireDraftAI](https://hiredraftai.com/)**.
